My Toolkit · Engineering depth in practice

From shop-floor cutting mechanics to structured decisions.

A dedicated collection of browser-based machining, tooling, and leadership calculators built from my cutting-tool engineering foundation—each pre-loaded with a worked example, governing formulas, and shop-floor caveats.

My Toolkit turns metal-cutting physics and manufacturing economics into interactive calculators you can inspect and test.

In machining and cutting-tool engineering, small parameter shifts carry steep trade-offs: raising cutting speed boosts removal rate but compresses insert life along the Taylor curve; choosing a speed by habit rather than the Engineered Productivity window leaves either machine capacity or edge life on the table.

Every calculator inside My Toolkit opens in your browser with a realistic worked example already populated—alongside step-by-step usage guidance, explicit equations (specific cutting force kc, MRR, Taylor V·Tⁿ = C, and minimum-cost vs. maximum-production tool life), and practical “watch out for” boundary checks.

While anchored in machining and cutting-tool engineering, the broader toolkit connects directly into Engineering, Operations, Leadership, and Communication decision aids—9 machining calculators in My Toolkit plus 6 additional catalog & repository entries (7 total catalog entries) hosted openly on GitHub Pages.
